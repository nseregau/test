How to Use

Golf Pro Tempo helps you perfect your golf swing rhythm with customizable audio cues.

Main Features

1.

Game Types

Choose between Long (3:1), Short (2:1), or set your own Custom ratio to match your natural swing.

2.

Tempo Control

Select from 8 preset speeds or create a Custom tempo to find your ideal rhythm.

3.

Sound Options

Choose from multiple sound types (Piano, Guitar, Drum, Beep, Voice, etc.) to find what works best for your training.

4.

Loop Settings

Set the pause between swing cycles. A 1-second base pause plus your selected interval value.

Training Tips

1.

Listen & Feel

Focus on matching your movement to the audio cues until it becomes automatic.

2.

Progressive Training

Start with slower tempos and gradually increase speed as your muscle memory develops.
